# Ten rules to take home

::left::

1. Time costs more than material
2. Buy or assemble before you machine
3. Tools are round and short
4. Fewer faces, fewer setups
5. Standard holes, few screw sizes

::right::

6. Tolerance only functional features
7. Adjust instead of demanding precision
8. Choose material for the process and the lab
9. Finishing is part of the design
10. Talk to the machinist early
