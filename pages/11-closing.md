# Exercise: redesign a scientist design

---

# The part

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-10 mt-4">

<div>

### A typical scientist design

- A **monolithic** 120 × 80 × 60 mm aluminum block
- **Sharp** internal corners everywhere
- A 5 mm wide pocket, **40 mm deep**
- Features on **five faces**
- **±0.01 mm** on every dimension
- M2.5, M3, M4, and M5 threads
- Sent as a STEP file only

# The exercise

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-10 mt-4">

<div>

### In teams (30–45 min)

1. List every feature that drives **cost**
2. **Redesign** it: same function, cheaper to make
3. Mark the **functional** features and tolerance only those
4. Make a **sketch** a machinist could quote from

### Then

The machinist estimates the **time** for the original and each redesign. We compare.

# Ten rules to take home

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-x-12 gap-y-1 mt-6">

<div>

1. Time costs more than material
2. Buy or assemble before you machine
3. Tools are round and short
4. Fewer faces, fewer setups
5. Standard holes, few screw sizes

</div>

<div>

6. Tolerance only functional features
7. Adjust instead of demanding precision
8. Choose material for the process and the lab
9. Finishing is part of the design
10. Talk to the machinist early

</div>
